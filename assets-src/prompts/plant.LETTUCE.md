# Plant Adult Stage Prompt Template (Fruiting / Mature)

# Base Style Block (Plantin 2.5D Stylized 3D)

Render an isolated 3D stylized game asset in a cute, polished cartoon style ("clay" / casual game render).

### Visual Aesthetics & Style:
- **Style**: Soft stylized 3D with chunky, smooth, rounded shapes and friendly proportions. Thick leaves, fleshy fruits, robust stems. Not hyper-realistic, no photographic textures, no micro-pores, no surface noise or grime.
- **Material**: Smooth matte or satin clay finish with subtle, soft specular highlights on fruits and glossy parts.
- **Lighting**: Soft studio lighting coming from the top-left (NW), casting gentle gradients and delicate ambient occlusion in crevices and joints. No harsh pitch-black shadows.
- **Camera & Perspective**: 3/4 isometric / dimetric angle from above (~30 degrees elevation, 2:1 dimetric ratio). Looking slightly downward. The object should match the perspective and orientation of the provided reference guide.
- **Background**: Solid, completely flat uniform background color with ZERO gradients, ZERO ground plane, ZERO floor, and NO cast shadow on the ground (the subject must float cleanly above the canvas).
- **Framing**: Centered in the frame with comfortable breathing room (10-15% padding from borders). Nothing cut off.
- **Root Collar & Grounding (Cuello de raíz)**: The plant base must end organically as a natural, intact living plant collar (cuello de tallo/corona radicular natural) that grounds firmly downward into the bottom center. DO NOT render severed, sliced, or chopped stem cross-sections (no cut cucumber/pipe look).
- **Strictly Forbidden**: NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO watermarks, NO pots or dirt (unless this is a pot asset), NO severed/sliced cut stem ends, NO floating stems, NO people, NO gardening tools, NO pedestals, NO background scenery, NO depth of field blur (everything sharp in focus).


### Subject Specification:
- **Crop**: Lechuga (LETTUCE)
- **Archetype**: Roseta Foliar Basal / Brássica & Hortaliza de Hoja
- **Stage**: Full adult maturity / Fruiting (BBCH 70–89).
- **Botanical Structure**: roseta basal densa y compacta de hojas anchas abanicadas verde tierno vibrante con bordes arrepollados ondulados
- **Natural Root Collar & Grounding (Cuello de raíz / Base enraizada)**:
  - The plant must originate from a **natural, organic root crown / stem collar (cuello del tallo ligeramente ensanchado o redondeado)** centered firmly at the bottom baseline.
  - **CRITICAL ANTI-PATTERN**: NEVER render a severed, flat, chopped, or sliced cross-section of a cylinder stem (it must NOT look like a cut flower stalk or sliced cucumber floating in the air). The stem base must look like an intact, living plant base ready to root into the soil, touching down vertically at the bottom center.
  - For vining plants (cucurbits/squash): the central rooted crown must sit grounded at the bottom center point, with runners and fruit resting naturally on the ground plane beside it.
- **Key Color for Background**: Solid `#FF00FF` background (completely flat and uniform).
